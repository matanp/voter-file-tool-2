# 05 - Internal party ballot generation

Status: needs-info

Part of [leader-feedback-2026-11-21](../README.md).

## Ask (verbatim)

> I don't know if you have this somewhere in the notes, but ballot generation should be somewhere in there at some point. I can help design various templates for internal party ballots to help them be pregenerated for committee meetings

We replied that we'll need more information.

## What was asked

Generate ballots for internal party votes at committee meetings, from templates, so they are ready before the
meeting. The stakeholder offered to help design the templates.

## Waiting on

The stakeholder's input on the templates. Questions to ask:

- What is voted on: officer elections, endorsements, designations, resolutions?
- What does a ballot contain: candidates or options, the committee or ED, instructions?
- Are ballots per member, and are they weighted? Weights already exist per seat
  ([leader-feedback-2026-11/02](../../leader-feedback-2026-11/issues/02-weights-per-seat-and-weight-report.md)).
- Who generates them: Admins only, or Leaders for their own committee?
- Is the output a PDF from the report server, like the sign-in sheet?

Once the templates are known, read `skills/adding-reports/SKILL.md`; this will likely be a new report type.
