## Resolution

Decided 2026-10-25 ([ADR 0001](../../adr/0001-committee-residency-by-assembly-district.md)):

- A voter may serve on a committee when they live in the committee's assembly district. Their town, LD and ED don't matter, so **Home ED may differ from Serve ED**. Option A is rejected.
- Option B isn't needed: Serve ED stays `CommitteeList.electionDistrict` and Home ED stays `VoterRecord.electionDistrict`, so no `serveEd` field is added.
- Enforcement points: the admission gate (`validateEligibility` in `apps/frontend/src/lib/eligibility.ts`) and the BOE standing audit (`runBoeEligibilityFlagging` in `packages/shared-prisma/src/boeEligibilityFlagging.ts`) both call the residency module `packages/shared-prisma/src/committeeResidency.ts`. A failure is the `ASSEMBLY_DISTRICT_MISMATCH` hard stop, checked only when `requireAssemblyDistrictMatch` is on.
- Follow-ups: the "Only eligible candidates" search filter still filters on town, LD and ED. It moves to the assembly district in `.scratch/committee-residency-rule/issues/04-eligible-only-filter-by-ad.md` and `05-addcommitteeform-path.md`.
- `SRS_GAPS_AND_CONSIDERATIONS.md` item 5.3 is now **Resolved**.
